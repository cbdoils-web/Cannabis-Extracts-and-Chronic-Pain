# Cannabis-Extracts-and-Chronic-Pain
Cannabis extracts contain cannabinoids such as THC and CBD, which are being studied for their potential role in pain management. 

Some research suggests that certain cannabis based treatments may help relieve specific types of chronic pain, although results vary depending on the product and individual.

 Understanding cannabinoid content, product quality, possible side effects, and medical guidance is essential when exploring these options.

 Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before using cannabis for pain.
